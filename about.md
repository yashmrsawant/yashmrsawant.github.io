### Core Philosophy

Understanding intelligence—whether in biological brains or mathematical and physical systems—requires bridging rigorous analytical theory with deep geometric and physical intuition. At **Scith**, every exposition is guided by three principles:

1. **First-Principles Mathematical Rigor:**  
   We do not treat equations as black boxes. Every theorem, integral transform, and distributional identity is derived step-by-step from foundational axioms so the reader sees *why* it must be true.

2. **Visceral Geometric &amp; Physical Intuition:**  
   Before diving into algebra, we ground every mathematical object in clear geometric pictures, interactive visualizations, and physical dimensions.

3. **Unifying Dynamics, Signals &amp; Intelligence:**  
   We connect pure harmonic analysis, differential equations, and probabilistic inference directly to how neural circuits and intelligent systems encode, transform, and compute over time.
