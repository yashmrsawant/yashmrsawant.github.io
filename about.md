### Biography

I am a PhD Candidate in Mathematical Sciences. My work focuses on synthesizing theoretical mathematical formalisms with concrete computational experiments to explore complex real-world data and dynamic physical phenomena.

On this site (**Scith**), I write rigorous, accessible expositions covering foundational mathematics, computational algorithms, and their scientific applications in neuroscience, signal processing, and machine learning.

---

### Research Interests

* **Harmonic Analysis & Signal Processing**  
  Continuous-time and discrete Fourier transforms, time-frequency distributions, wavelets, and spectral estimation techniques for non-stationary signals.

* **Computational Neuroscience & Brain Modelling**  
  Mathematical modeling of neural voltage recordings, neural field equations, dynamical systems in cortical networks, and decoding complex bio-signals.

* **Machine Learning & Applied Mathematics**  
  Mathematical foundations of deep representations, high-dimensional probability, Gaussian integrals, calculus of variations, and optimization geometry.

---

### Methodological Approach

Scientific insight is strongest when **analytical derivation** and **computational validation** go hand in hand:

1. **Analytical Grounding**: Deriving properties directly from first principles (integral transforms, convergence proofs, Gaussian evaluations).
2. **Computational Verification**: Implementing the theory in clean, reproducible code (NumPy, SciPy, PyTorch) to test theoretical edge cases.
3. **Application to Real Systems**: Applying mathematical tools to real neurobiological time-series and cognitive signal data.
